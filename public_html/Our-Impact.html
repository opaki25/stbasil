Our Impact

Care that restores dignity, comfort, and hope.

St. Basil Palliative Home Care Promotion supports vulnerable people and families facing serious illness through home-based care, counselling, spiritual accompaniment, health guidance, and community support.

Our impact is seen in families who feel less alone, patients who receive compassionate attention, and communities that better understand palliative care as a pathway of comfort and dignity.

Support This Work

Home Care

Families receive care guidance and psychosocial support where patients live.

Whole-Person Support

Patients are supported physically, emotionally, socially, and spiritually.

Family Guidance

Caregivers are helped to understand illness, comfort care, and family roles.

Community Action

Education and outreach strengthen awareness of palliative care in the community.

What Changes

The difference our care seeks to make

Palliative care is not only medical attention. It is a steady presence that helps people live with less pain, less fear, and more dignity.

01

Patients feel seen

Through listening, visits, prayer, counselling, and care planning, patients are treated as whole persons with dignity.

02

Caregivers feel supported

Families receive practical guidance and emotional support as they care for loved ones through difficult seasons.

03

Communities understand care

Education and outreach help reduce fear and stigma around serious illness, aging, disability, and end-of-life support.
