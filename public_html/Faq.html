Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about St. Basil Palliative Home Care Promotion, our home care support, donations, and how families can contact the team for help.

We provide compassionate palliative home care support for people facing serious or life-limiting illness. Our work includes family visits, counselling, spiritual care, health guidance, and practical community support.

Families, caregivers, community members, and patients in need of palliative care support can contact us. We especially focus on vulnerable people affected by chronic illness, disability, cancer, HIV/AIDS, stroke, diabetes, old age, and other serious conditions.

Our central office is located at Euata, Ragem community, about 5 kilometers from Arua City headquarters along Nebbi-Pakwach road.

You can call us on +256 772 994 886 or +256 772 532 284. You can also email us at stbasilpalliativehomecareltd@gmail.com or use the contact page to send a message.

You can support St. Basil through donations, prayer, professional volunteering, community referrals, and partnerships that help us reach more families with care and dignity.
